# CaseSaathi AI

### AI-Powered Legal Case Guidance & Citizen Assistance Portal

**CaseSaathi AI** is a web-based legal assistance platform designed to help ordinary citizens understand complex legal information in simpler language, organize case-related documents, and stay informed about court hearings and important deadlines.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Many people find it difficult to understand complex court documents, legal terminology, and case-related procedures. Keeping track of hearing dates and organizing important documents can also be challenging.

CaseSaathi AI aims to simplify access to legal information and help users manage their case-related information more effectively.

## 💡 Our Solution

CaseSaathi AI provides a user-friendly interface for exploring legal information and organizing case details. It aims to make legal information easier to navigate and understand.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Dashboard:** Overview of cases, analyzed documents, pending hearings, and urgent deadlines.
- **Explain Document:** Designed to help users understand complex legal documents in simpler language.
- **My Cases / Case Dossiers:** Organize case titles, reference numbers, court details, and related information.
- **Cause List & Hearings:** Interface for reviewing hearing-related information and schedules.
- **Legal Glossary & Aid:** Access legal terminology and citizen assistance information.
- **Bilingual Interface:** Hindi and English interface elements.
- **Accessibility Options:** Text-size controls and display preferences.

*Note: Actual functionality depends on the current implementation.*
